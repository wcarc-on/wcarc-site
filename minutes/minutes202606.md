### 2026 June VHF Contest Post-Mortem Discussion

An informal discussion between the members took place. Suggestions for next year's contest were noted. One key point was the safety and coordination requirements during tower erection.

### VHF Contest Post-Mortem discussion

- Bert thanked all participants in our June VHF Contest outing. The turnout was good, so setup and tear-down went smoothly. The stations were also fully manned for almost all of the contest duration. The renovated Corkery Community Centre was also found to be excellent for our activity.
- Roger Cormier lost a small size, black finish socket adapter tool. Please look into your toolbox!

### Old Business

- Tuesday evening SSB net: Lawrence (VA3IQ) will take over the duties once his antenna stack and mast are installed, which is currently happening. He is planning on resuming the Net in September, and will be active on four bands and with 25 Watts of output power. Shared net control duties is also considered. __Open item__.
- Mike (VA3TEC) to report back on the Club's finances at the July meeting. __Open item__
- Club membership list: Ted (VE3TD) had asked for a membership list for AREDN mapping purpose. Mike (VA3TEC) produced that list and provide it to Ted. __Closed item__

### New Business

- Lawrence (VA3IQ) mentioned the availability of the Corkery Centre as a location for our Christmas party. Rental fee for the conference room and the kitchen would be $50 for the evening. We will discuss and decide whether to reserve the venue at the July meeting. __Open item__

### Show and tell / Round Table

- Bert (VE2ZAZ) confirmed the end of CHU-Canada transmissions. He published a video on YouTube that captures the exact moment the 3,330 KHz transmitter was turned off. That link was posted onto the Club's Facebook page.

### Adjournment

The President observed that there was no further business. Bert (VE2ZAZ) moved that the meeting be adjourned. Lawrence (VA3IQ) seconded the motion. The meeting was adjourned at 9:06 pm.
